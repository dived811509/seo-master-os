## What is a Marketing Automation Workflow and How Does It Work?

A **marketing automation workflow** is a step-by-step flowchart of automatic marketing actions (such as sending an email, giving a discount code, tagging a customer, or waiting a couple of days), triggered by what a customer does on your store.

Creating custom workflows from scratch can feel overwhelming. You have to map out logic trees, set up complex trigger conditions, and test everything manually. ### 1. The Welcome Series: Convert New Subscribers into First-Time Buyers

**Goal:** Welcome new subscribers warmly and guide them toward making their first purchase within 48 hours.

When someone signs up on your store popup, their interest in your brand is at an all-time high. A 3-step welcome sequence introduces your brand story, shares customer proof, and delivers an immediate welcome discount.

#### Visual Workflow Blueprint
```
[User Subscribes via Popup]
           │
           ▼
 (Send Instant Email 1: Welcome + Discount Code)
           │
           ▼
    (Wait 48 Hours)
           │
           ▼
 (Send Email 2: Brand Story & Values)
           │
           ▼
    (Wait 72 Hours)
           │
           ▼
 (Send Email 3: Bestselling Products Showcase)
```

#### Email 1 Copy Template (Instant Welcome)
- **Subject Line:** `Welcome to [Brand Name]! Here’s your 10% off inside 🎁`
- **Email Body Snippet:**
  > *"Hey [First Name],*  
  > *Welcome to the [Brand Name] family! We’re so glad you’re here.*  
  > *As a quick thank-you, use code **WELCOME10** at checkout to get 10% off your very first order.*  
  > *[Shop Bestsellers Now Button]"*

#### Email 2 Copy Template (Brand Story & Values)
- **Subject Line:** `Behind the scenes: Why we started [Brand Name]`
- **Email Body Snippet:**
  > *"Hi [First Name],*  
  > *We founded [Brand Name] with a simple mission: to create high-quality products that make a real difference in your daily routine.*  
  > *Every item we make is built with care, sustainable materials, and a commitment to quality.*  
  > *Your 10% discount is still active! Use code **WELCOME10** today.*  
  > *[Discover Our Story Button]"*

#### Email 3 Copy Template (Bestsellers Showcase & Proof)
- **Subject Line:** `Customer favorites: What everyone is loving right now 🔥`
- **Email Body Snippet:**
  > *"Hey [First Name],*

### 4. The VIP Loyalty Flow: Reward Your Best Customers

**Goal:** Reward your top repeat spenders so they feel valued and keep coming back.

Your best buyers deserve special treatment. Customers who place 3+ orders or cross a specific spending milestone should be automatically moved into an exclusive VIP flow.

#### Visual Workflow Blueprint
```
[Customer Reaches 3+ Orders]
           │
           ▼
 (Tag Customer as VIP in System)
           │
           ▼
```

### 5. The Win-Back Flow: Re-Engage Lapsed Shoppers

**Goal:** Gently re-engage customers who haven't ordered in 60 to 90 days.

Getting a new customer costs 5x more than keeping an existing one. A win-back flow automatically spots quiet accounts and invites them back with a special re-engagement discount.

#### Visual Workflow Blueprint
```
[No Purchase Activity for 60 Days]
           │
           ▼
 (Send Email 1: "We Miss You" + 15% Off)
           │
           ▼
    (Wait 7 Days - Did They Buy?)
      ├── YES ──> [Move to Active List]
      └── NO  ──> [Send Email 2: Final Chance Offer]
```

- **Subject Line:** `We miss you, [First Name]! Here’s $10 off your next order`

---

### 6. The Browse Abandonment Flow: Convert Window Shoppers into Buyers

**Goal:** Remind visitors who checked out specific products without placing them in their cart.

Browse abandonment automations catch shopper interest early. If a logged-in subscriber looks at a product page twice in a day, a helpful recommendation email goes out.

- **Subject Line:** `Eyeing something special? Take another look 👀`
- **Tip:** Share customer reviews, star ratings, and real photos of the product they viewed.

---

### 7. The Review Request Flow: Collect Social Proof and Reviews Automatically

**Goal:** Collect real product reviews and photo feedback on autopilot.

Customer reviews build trust for future shoppers. Sending your review request 10 to 14 days after delivery ensures your customer has had time to try their product.

#### Visual Workflow Blueprint
```
[Order Delivered Event] ──> (Wait 14 Days) ──> [Send Email: Rate Your Purchase & Share a Photo]
```

- **Subject Line:** `How did we do? Share your feedback (and get 10% off)`
- **Incentive:** Offer reward points or a discount code for leaving a review with a photo.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Marketing Automation Workflows

### What is the difference between a drip campaign and a marketing automation workflow?
A drip campaign sends emails on fixed days regardless of user actions. A marketing automation workflow changes messages, timing, and offers in real time based on live customer behavior.

### How many emails should be in an abandoned cart workflow?
The most effective abandoned cart recovery workflow uses two to three emails: a friendly reminder after one hour, a review proof email after 24 hours, and a final discount.
