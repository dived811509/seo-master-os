# Keyword Cannibalization Analysis: Homepage vs /email-marketing/

**Target Domain:** `adflipr.com`  
**Analyzed By:** SEO MASTER AGENT (SEO Intelligence & Analytics Persona)  
**Date:** September 04, 2026  
**Workspace:** Sub-project: Dived  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

**User Query/Concern:**  
*"My home page primary keyword is 'Email Marketing Automation'. Now my concern is: do 'Email Marketing Automation' and 'Ecommerce Email Marketing Software' have the same intent, creating a keyword cannibalization issue between Homepage (`/`) and Service Page (`/email-marketing/`)?"*

### **Direct Diagnosis: YES, HIGH CANNIBALIZATION RISK EXISTS**
If the **Homepage (`/`)** and the **Service Page (`/email-marketing/`)** both target broad terms combining *Ecommerce*, *Email*, *Software*, and *Automation*, Google will be unable to determine which page is the primary canonical authority. This leads to ranking fluctuations, split PageRank/backlink signals, and suppressed positions for both pages.

## 3. Structural Keyword & URL Mapping Strategy (Siloing)

To eliminate keyword cannibalization completely, each page on `adflipr.com` must have a **distinct intent boundary and unique primary keyword assignment**:

```
adflipr.com (Homepage - Brand Umbrella / All-in-One Platform)
│
├── /email-marketing/  ──► Primary: "Ecommerce Email Marketing Software" (Focus: Campaigns, Builder, Templates)
│
├── /automations/      ──► Primary: "Ecommerce Email Marketing Automation" (Focus: Workflows, Triggers, Flows)
│
└── /abandoned-cart-recovery/ ──► Primary: "Ecommerce Abandoned Cart Recovery Tool" (Focus: Cart Recovery)
```

### **1. Homepage (`/`) Mapping Strategy**
* **Role:** Brand Homepage & All-in-One eCommerce Marketing Platform Umbrella.
* **Primary Keyword:** `eCommerce Email & Marketing Automation Platform`
* **Title Tag:** `Adflipr | All-in-One Email Marketing Software for eCommerce`
* **H1 Heading:** `Automate Your eCommerce Growth with Smart Email Marketing & Workflows`

### **2. Service Page (`/email-marketing/`) Mapping Strategy**
* **Role:** Product Pillar for Email Campaigns, Newsletter Builder & Design.
* **Primary Keyword:** `Ecommerce Email Marketing Software`
* **Secondary Keywords:** `Ecommerce Email Campaign Tool`, `Drag and Drop Email Builder for Online Stores`
* **Title Tag:** `Ecommerce Email Marketing Software & Campaign Tool | Adflipr`
* **H1 Heading:** `Ecommerce Email Marketing Software Built to Drive Repeat Sales`

### **3. Dedicated Automations Page (`/automations/`) Mapping Strategy**
* **Role:** Product Pillar for Automated Workflows & Trigger-based Journeys.
* **Primary Keyword:** `Email Marketing Automation for Ecommerce`
* **Secondary Keywords:** `Ecommerce Email Automation Tool`, `Automated Email Sequences for Shopify`
* **Title Tag:** `eCommerce Email Marketing Automation & Workflow Builder | Adflipr`
* **H1 Heading:** `Turn Visitors into Repeat Buyers with Automated eCommerce Email Flows`

## 4. Internal Linking Rules to Prevent Cannibalization

1. **Contextual Anchors:** When linking from blog posts or external content to `/email-marketing/`, use exact anchor variations like `ecommerce email marketing software` or `email campaign builder`.
2. **Automation Anchors:** When linking to `/automations/`, use anchors like `email marketing automation` or `automated email flows`.
3. **Homepage Anchors:** Reserve brand anchors (`Adflipr`, `Adflipr ecommerce platform`) for internal links pointing to the root domain (`/`).
