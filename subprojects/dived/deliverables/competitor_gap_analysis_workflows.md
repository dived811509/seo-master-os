## 2. EVIDENCE (Competitor Strengths & Weaknesses Breakdown)

### 🔴 Competitor 1: Bloomreach
- **What They Do Well:**
  - Includes a clean "Key Takeaways" box at the top.
  - Implements FAQ accordion schema.
  - Covers omnichannel concepts (email, SMS, web personalization).
- **Where They Are Lacking:**
  - Written for enterprise enterprise CDPs; completely ignores SMB e-commerce store needs.
  - No actionable email copy templates or downloadable workflow structures.
  - Lacks visual trigger/condition flowcharts.

### 🔴 Competitor 2: Insider (useinsider.com)
- **What They Do Well:**
  - Strong listicle structure ("9 proven workflows").
  - Includes screenshots of their platform UI.
- **Where They Are Lacking:**
  - Overly focused on mobile app push notifications rather than core email marketing automations.
  - Ignores Shopify/WooCommerce platform integration triggers.
  - Gives vague descriptions without step-by-step delay & condition logic.

### 🔴 Competitor 3: Aprimo
- **What They Do Well:**
  - Detailed operational workflow coverage for content production.
- **Where They Are Lacking:**
  - Confuses customer-facing marketing automation with internal Digital Asset Management (DAM).
  - Low relevance for e-commerce merchants looking to automate customer retention and sales.
