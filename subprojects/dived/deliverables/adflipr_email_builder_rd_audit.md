## 1. FINDING
The `https://adflipr.com/email-builder/` service page is currently optimized around **"drag and drop email builder"** in its title tag and meta description, but lacks strategic focus on high-intent e-commerce and pre-send auditing intent modifiers. While **"drag and drop email builder"** is the highest volume primary search term for this service category, the current content misses opportunities to capture high-converting niche searches such as **"ecommerce email builder"**, **"no code email builder"**, and **"shopify email builder"**.

## 3. INTERPRETATION
* **Head Keyword Dominance:** `drag and drop email builder` is indisputably the highest volume primary keyword for this landing page. Targeting anything generic like "email builder" would pit the page against generic API platforms, whereas "drag and drop email builder" targets exact feature intent.
* **Content Unique Selling Proposition (USP):** Adflipr is not just a generic HTML builder—it features **native Shopify/WooCommerce product & cart blocks** plus an **automated Pre-Send Email Auditor**. 
* **Current Gap:** Competitors like Stripo, Beefree, and Unlayer compete heavily on generic template terms. Adflipr's content contains the technical capabilities, but the header hierarchy (H1/H2s) does not reinforce keyword variants like `e-commerce email template builder` or `no-code email builder for Shopify`.

---

## 4. OPPORTUNITY
1. **Primary Keyword Strategy:** Lock in **`drag and drop email builder`** as the primary head keyword across Title, H1, Meta Description, and schema markup.
2. **Secondary Keyword Integration:** Expand search reach by embedding high-intent long-tail keywords:
   - Primary: `drag and drop email builder`
   - Secondary 1: `no code email builder`
   - Secondary 2: `ecommerce email builder`
   - Secondary 3: `shopify email builder` / `woocommerce email builder`
   - Secondary 4: `email template auditor tool`
3. **Featured Snippet Opportunity:** Optimizing the **Pre-Send Email Auditor section** (8-point checklist: spam check, text-to-image ratio, missing URLs, broken links, email size, unsubscribe tag) into structured bullet points for snippet capture on queries like "how to audit email template before sending".
