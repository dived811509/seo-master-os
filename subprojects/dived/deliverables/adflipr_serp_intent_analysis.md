## 1. Executive Diagnostic: You Are 100% Correct!

**User Observation:**  
*"SERP is showing that the keyword performs good only for blog, not for the service page. Is it right?"*

**Diagnostic Finding:**  
**YES, YOUR OBSERVATION IS 100% ACCURATE.**

Google differentiates intent based on the presence of commercial modifiers:
* **Without Modifier (`eCommerce Email Marketing`):** Google interprets this as **Informational Intent**. Searchers want guides, strategies, and tutorials. Top ranking results are **Blog Posts** (Shopify Blog, HubSpot Blog, Mailchimp Guides).
* **With Commercial Modifier (`eCommerce Email Marketing Software` / `eCommerce Email Marketing Platform`):** Google interprets this as **Transactional/Commercial Intent**. Searchers want software features, integrations, and pricing. Top ranking results are **Service / Product Landing Pages** (Klaviyo, Omnisend, Brevo).
