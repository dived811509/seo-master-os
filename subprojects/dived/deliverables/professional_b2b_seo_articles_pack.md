### Section 2: Technical Mitigations for Cart Abandonment
Shopping cart abandonment remains the single largest point of revenue leakage in digital commerce, averaging over 70% globally. Primary friction points include unexpected checkout shipping fees, mandatory account creation, and checkout interface complexity.

To counteract abandonment leakage, leading retailers deploy three-stage automated recovery sequences:

1. **Phase I (1-2 Hours Post-Abandonment):** A responsive, brand-aligned email confirming item reservation without commercial pressure.
2. **Phase II (24 Hours Post-Abandonment):** Incorporating dynamic social proof, customer ratings, and answers to common product inquiries.
3. **Phase III (48 Hours Post-Abandonment):** Providing time-sensitive incentives or inventory threshold notifications to trigger checkout completion.

- Unexpected shipping calculations at final checkout.
- Complex multi-step registration forms.
- Lack of visible security assurances and payment options.
- Website performance latency on mobile devices.
- Price comparison behavior without intent to purchase immediately.
