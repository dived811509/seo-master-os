## Detailed Blog-by-Blog SEO Analysis & Solutions

---

### Blog 01: Email List Management (`01-mailing-list-best-practices.md`)
- **Primary Keyword:** `email list management` (Est. MSV: **2,900 – 4,400**)
- **Secondary Keywords:** `email list hygiene`, `mailing lists`, `email list`
- **NLP Entities:** `GDPR`, `CASL`, `double opt-in`, `sender reputation`, `hard/soft bounce rate`, `spam traps`, `engagement ratio`.
- **Rating:** `9.0 / 10`
- **Strengths:** Clear 5-point structure, legal compliance touchpoints, real mathematical engagement rate formula.
- **Problem / Gap:** The secondary keyword `email list hygiene` is critical for high-intent B2B searchers, but it only appears in body paragraphs.
- **Solution:** Update H2 heading from `Clean Your List on a Regular Schedule` to `Email List Hygiene: Clean Your List on a Regular Schedule`.

### Blog 02: Email Marketing Campaign Examples (`02-email-marketing-campaign-examples.md`)
- **Primary Keyword:** `email marketing campaign examples` (Est. MSV: **3,600 – 5,400**)
- **Secondary Keywords:** `email campaign templates`, `drip email campaign`, `email campaign ideas`
- **NLP Entities:** `Welcome series`, `abandoned cart`, `post-purchase`, `replenishment`, `win-back`, `VIP loyalty`, `seasonal sends`.
- **Rating:** `9.2 / 10`
- **Strengths:** Strong listicle structure, clear prioritization framework (Transactional > Lifecycle > Broad).

- **Solution:** Either adjust H1 to `7 Essential Email Marketing Campaign Examples for Ecommerce Stores` OR add sub-numbered examples (1.1, 1.2) within each category to reach 15 concrete examples.

### ⚠️ Blog 13: Benchmarking Email Marketing (`13-how-to-benchmark-email-marketing-performance.md`)
- **Primary Keyword in File:** `benchmarking` (⚠️ **SEO CRITIQUE: TOO BROAD!**)
- **Required Primary Keyword:** **`email marketing benchmarks`** / `benchmarking email marketing` (Est. MSV: **2,900 – 4,400**)
- **Secondary Keywords:** `email marketing benchmarks`, `ecommerce benchmark`
- **NLP Entities:** `Click-to-Open Rate (CTOR)`, `Apple Mail Privacy Protection (MPP)`, `historical baseline`, `automated flows vs broadcasts`.
- **Rating:** `7.8 / 10`
- **Critical Flaw:** The metadata keyphrase is listed as `benchmarking`. `Benchmarking` alone is ambiguous (financial benchmarking, software benchmarking, IT benchmarking). Google cannot determine topic specialization if the primary keyphrase is a single generic word.
- **Solution:** Change `Keyphrase: benchmarking` to `Keyphrase: email marketing benchmarks` in Yoast SEO metadata and update H1 to `Benchmarking Email Marketing Performance: How to Compare Against Industry Benchmarks`.
