Implementing timely multi-step cart recovery workflows recovers 15--25\% of lost checkout sessions. Key benchmarks include:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Sequence 1 (1 Hour):} Friendly reminder with cart contents.
    \item \textbf{Sequence 2 (24 Hours):} Value proposition and customer reviews.
    \item \textbf{Sequence 3 (48 Hours):} Incentive/discount offer.
\end{itemize}
