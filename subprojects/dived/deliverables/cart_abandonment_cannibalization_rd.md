## 🎯 VARIATION 1: The Automated Email Recovery Angle (RECOMMENDED)

### **Primary Keyword:** `Abandoned Cart Email` (Est. MSV: **4,400 – 6,600+**)
- **New Strategic Objective:** Transform Blog B into the ultimate **Abandoned Cart Email Sequence & Copy Guide**. Instead of discussing general website design, focus 100% on automated follow-up emails, sequence timing, subject line ideas, and copy templates.
- **Secondary Keywords:** `abandoned cart recovery email` (2,900 MSV), `abandoned cart email examples` (1,900 MSV), `abandoned cart email subject lines` (1,300 MSV).
- **Proposed H1 Title:** `8 Abandoned Cart Email Strategies to Recover Lost Sales Automatically`
- **Why this wins for Adflipr:** Direct link to your commercial product landing page ([abandoned-cart-recovery](https://adflipr.com/abandoned-cart-recovery/))! Completely eliminates keyword cannibalization with Blog A.
